# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** start a thread in this repository's Discussions.
- **Something is broken:** open an issue with the bug form.
- **Security problem:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
- **Your server, account or library:** ask whoever runs your Jellyfin server.
  Zorofin is a client and can't see or change your server's settings.

Setup guides and app downloads are on [zorofin.app](https://zorofin.app).
